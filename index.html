A helpdesk inside your Flarum forum.

A knowledge base and private support tickets, built on Flarum's own discussions, tags, permissions and notifications. Your members get help where they already are, and visitors without an account can reach you too.

Everything a small support team needs

No separate helpdesk app, no second login. Tickets and articles are Flarum discussions, so search, mentions, formatting and moderation all work as usual.

Knowledge base

Pick the tags that hold your articles. Members get a searchable knowledge base page with categories, and articles can be kept out of All Discussions.

Private tickets

Only the person who opened a ticket and your support staff can see it. Tickets never show up in discussion lists or tag pages.

Status & priority

Open, pending, resolved and closed, with low, normal and high priority. Every change is recorded in the ticket's timeline.

Multiple assignees

Assign one or several staff members. Filter the ticket list by status, priority, "assigned to me" or unassigned.

Notifications & email

New tickets, staff replies, customer replies and status changes, in the forum and by email. Each user picks what they receive.

Permissions

Separate permissions for viewing the knowledge base, opening tickets and managing tickets, set per group in Flarum's admin panel.

Tickets from visitors without an account

People who can't log in are often the ones who need help most. Turn on guest tickets and visitors get a contact form on /tickets.

  • Email confirmation. A request becomes a ticket only after the visitor opens the link emailed to them. Unconfirmed requests are deleted after 7 days.
  • Secret links, no passwords. Guests read and answer their ticket through a link in each email. The token stays in the URL fragment and only its hash is stored.
  • Spam protection. Rate limits per IP and per email address, plus a honeypot field.
  • Privacy built in. Staff see the guest's name and email; deleting the ticket deletes them too.
  • Off by default. Enable it with one switch in the extension settings.

How installing works

Your purchase gives your GitHub account read access to the private repository. Composer installs it from there like any other extension.

Buy and connect GitHub

After checkout, connect your GitHub account on the purchase page. You'll get an invitation to the private repository; accept it.

Give Composer access

Create a classic GitHub token with the repo scope, then on your server:

composer config repositories.flarum-support vcs https://github.com/Soup-97/pixmmo-flarum-support-system
composer config github-oauth.github.com YOUR_TOKEN

Install

composer require soup-97/flarum-support
php flarum migrate
php flarum cache:clear

Then enable Support in your admin panel.

Questions

Which Flarum versions are supported?

Flarum 2.x. The extension needs PHP 8.3 or newer, MySQL or MariaDB, and the bundled Tags extension.

Do I need a GitHub account?

Yes. Access to the code and updates goes through GitHub, so you need a (free) GitHub account to buy and install.

What happens when my subscription ends?

You can keep using the last version you received. You won't get new versions or support until you renew.

Can I change the code?

Yes, for use on your own forum. You may not redistribute or resell it. The translation files are MIT-licensed, so you can share translations freely.

How do I get support?

Once you have access, open an issue in the private repository. Before buying, ask in the extension's thread on discuss.flarum.org.
